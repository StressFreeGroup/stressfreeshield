Coverage details

What's covered — and what's not

Items not expressly listed as covered are excluded. All claims are subject to estimate review and approval prior to reimbursement.

✓
What IS covered
Accidental interior damage while vehicle is stationaryNot from negligence, misuse, or willful acts
Refrigerator or freezer handle breakage
Microwave turntable plate (glass plate only)Not the microwave unit itself
Interior sliding door off trackNot applicable to slide-out room doors
Toilet clog — standard clearingForeign-object blockage requiring pump-out excluded
Minor marks, scrapes, or scuffs — max 3 inches, one location per occurrence
Interior hardware: cabinet latches, magnetic catches, drawer pulls, hinges, handle screws
Window blind slats or pull cords — accidental breakage
Shower curtain rod or hooks — accidental breakage
Broken cup holders or small molded interior trim components
Fresh water hose and grommetNEW
Septic hose and adaptorNEW
Stabilizer jack hand-crankNEW
TV remote — missing or brokenNEWRemote only — not the TV or entertainment unit
Sink, shower, or tub faucet fixtures — accidental damageNEW
One courtesy mobile service callIf provided by host at host's sole discretion
Missing interior supply kit items (small bin items only)
Scratch & Scuff Limit: Marks up to 3 inches, one location per occurrence only.
✕
What is NOT covered
Pet or animal damage of any kind
Tires, slide-outs, or awnings
Exterior body, paint, glass, or roof
Generator or mechanical systems
TV, audio, or entertainment system units
Keys, fobs, or lock hardware
Acts of nature or acts of God
Negligence, misuse, or rental agreement violations
Water damage from open vents or windows
Theft of any items
Damage while vehicle is in motion
Pre-existing or undisclosed damage
Foreign-object toilet blockage (pump-out required)
Festival or high-attendance event rentals
Claims submitted after the 5-day window
Sensor readings billable by host
Anything covered by another coverage program
🚫
Festival & high-attendance event exclusion

The Standard Shield does not apply to any event with 500+ expected attendees within 25 miles of the vehicle. Named exclusions: Burning Man, Coachella, Stagecoach, King of the Hammers, EDC, and any event designated by Stress Free RVs at booking.

Planning an event trip? Ask about Premium Event Coverage — available separately.
