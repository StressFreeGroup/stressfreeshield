Interior sliding door off track

A bathroom or bedroom sliding or pocket door that has come off its track.

$250 deductible per occurrenceUp to $2,500 per coverage durationParked (stationary) only

What it is

Many RVs use sliding or pocket doors for the bathroom and bedroom to save space. These doors hang on small rollers that run in a track, and a hard pull or slam can pop the rollers out so the door jams, drags, or falls loose.

Typical parts cost

Often fixed with an adjustment. Replacement rollers or guides commonly run about $10 to $30.

Photo examples

What the Shield covers

  • Re-seating an interior sliding or pocket door that has come off its track
  • Accidental damage to the door rollers or guides that keep it on the track

Not covered

  • Slide-out room doors and slide-out mechanisms
  • Exterior entry doors and locks
  • Doors damaged by force, misuse, or negligence

How RV and trailer rentals usually handle this

In the RV and trailer rental industry, small interior items like this are usually treated as the renter's responsibility. Rental agreements commonly list them on a damage or replacement-fee schedule and deduct the cost from the security deposit, while the larger protection plans offered with rentals are built around major physical damage and often carry deductibles higher than the repair itself. A damage waiver such as the Stress Free Shield is designed to fill that gap.

Off-track sliding doors are one of the most common RV service calls. Most are a quick adjustment rather than a replacement, which is why a review-first process often saves money.

Avoid it: Slide the door gently and evenly. If it sticks, stop and report it rather than forcing it.
