Accidental interior damage (while parked)

Sudden, unintentional damage inside the unit while it is stationary.

$250 deductible per occurrenceUp to $2,500 per coverage durationParked (stationary) only

What it is

Accidental interior damage is unexpected, unintentional damage to the inside of the RV or trailer that happens during normal use while the vehicle is parked, such as a part that snaps or comes loose. It is the umbrella that every other covered item on this list falls under.

Photo examples

What the Shield covers

  • Sudden, accidental damage to the covered interior items listed in the coverage schedule
  • Damage that happens while the vehicle is stationary (parked, leveled, or set up at camp)
  • Subject to the $250 deductible per occurrence and the $2,500 maximum per coverage duration

Not covered

  • Negligence, misuse, willful acts, or rental agreement violations
  • Damage that happens while the vehicle is in motion
  • Normal wear and tear, pre-existing damage, and pet or animal damage
  • Any item not expressly listed as covered

How RV and trailer rentals usually handle this

In the RV and trailer rental industry, small interior items like this are usually treated as the renter's responsibility. Rental agreements commonly list them on a damage or replacement-fee schedule and deduct the cost from the security deposit, while the larger protection plans offered with rentals are built around major physical damage and often carry deductibles higher than the repair itself. A damage waiver such as the Stress Free Shield is designed to fill that gap.

Rental agreements across the industry draw a line between normal wear and tear, which the owner absorbs, and accidental damage, which is typically billed to the renter after the post-trip inspection.

Avoid it: Walk through the unit at pickup and photograph anything already worn or marked, so it is never confused with new damage.
