Shower curtain rod or hooks

The rod or hooks that hold the shower curtain.

$250 deductible per occurrenceUp to $2,500 per coverage durationParked (stationary) only

What it is

Many RV showers use a tension rod, curved rod, or ceiling track with hooks or rings to hold the curtain. These pieces are lightweight and can bend, pop loose, or snap if pulled on or used for support.

Typical parts cost

Replacement rods and hook sets commonly run about $10 to $40.

Photo examples

What the Shield covers

  • Accidental breakage of the shower curtain rod or track
  • Accidental breakage of curtain hooks or rings

Not covered

  • Shower walls, pan, surround, or glass doors
  • Water damage
  • The shower curtain fabric itself

How RV and trailer rentals usually handle this

In the RV and trailer rental industry, small interior items like this are usually treated as the renter's responsibility. Rental agreements commonly list them on a damage or replacement-fee schedule and deduct the cost from the security deposit, while the larger protection plans offered with rentals are built around major physical damage and often carry deductibles higher than the repair itself. A damage waiver such as the Stress Free Shield is designed to fill that gap.

Rods and hooks are typically billed at replacement cost after inspection, since they are usually sized to the specific shower.

Avoid it: Do not hang wet towels or clothing on the rod, and never use it for balance.
