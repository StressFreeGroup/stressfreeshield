Microwave turntable plate

The round glass plate that spins inside the microwave.

$250 deductible per occurrenceUp to $2,500 per coverage durationParked (stationary) only

What it is

The turntable plate is the removable round glass tray that sits on a roller ring inside the microwave and rotates food while it heats. In RVs it is the part of the microwave most likely to break, because it is loose glass in a moving vehicle.

Typical parts cost

Replacement glass plates commonly run about $15 to $50, matched to the microwave model.

Photo examples

What the Shield covers

  • The glass turntable plate only, when broken accidentally while the vehicle is parked

Not covered

  • The microwave unit, its door, electronics, or controls
  • A plate that breaks while the vehicle is moving

How RV and trailer rentals usually handle this

In the RV and trailer rental industry, small interior items like this are usually treated as the renter's responsibility. Rental agreements commonly list them on a damage or replacement-fee schedule and deduct the cost from the security deposit, while the larger protection plans offered with rentals are built around major physical damage and often carry deductibles higher than the repair itself. A damage waiver such as the Stress Free Shield is designed to fill that gap.

Rental units commonly ask renters to remove and pad the glass plate before driving. Because breakage in transit is so common, rental damage schedules usually list the plate as a separate charge.

Avoid it: Before you drive, take the plate out and wrap it in a towel in a low cabinet. A plate broken in transit is not covered, because the Shield applies only while the vehicle is stationary.
