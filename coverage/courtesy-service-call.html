One courtesy mobile service call

One visit from a mobile technician, if the host chooses to provide it.

$250 deductible per occurrenceUp to $2,500 per coverage durationParked (stationary) only

What it is

A mobile service call is a visit from an RV technician to wherever the unit is parked, to diagnose or repair an issue on site instead of towing the RV to a shop. With the Shield, the host may provide one courtesy service call at the host's sole discretion.

Typical parts cost

Mobile trip fees are often around $100 to $200 before labor, depending on the market and distance.

Photo examples

What the Shield covers

  • One courtesy mobile service call, when provided by the host at the host's sole discretion

Not covered

  • Additional service calls beyond the first
  • Towing or roadside assistance
  • Repairs to items that are not covered

How RV and trailer rentals usually handle this

In the RV and trailer rental industry, small interior items like this are usually treated as the renter's responsibility. Rental agreements commonly list them on a damage or replacement-fee schedule and deduct the cost from the security deposit, while the larger protection plans offered with rentals are built around major physical damage and often carry deductibles higher than the repair itself. A damage waiver such as the Stress Free Shield is designed to fill that gap.

Mobile RV technicians commonly charge a trip or call-out fee before any labor or parts, which on its own can exceed the cost of a small repair.

Avoid it: Call or text the host first. They may route you to a pre-screened technician from the Stress Free Shield service network.
