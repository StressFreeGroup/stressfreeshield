Interior hardware

Cabinet latches, magnetic catches, drawer pulls, hinges, and handle screws.

$250 deductible per occurrenceUp to $2,500 per coverage durationParked (stationary) only

What it is

Interior hardware is the small metal and plastic hardware that keeps cabinets and drawers working: push-button or travel latches, magnetic catches, drawer pulls and knobs, cabinet hinges, and the screws that hold handles in place. RV cabinetry is lightweight, so this hardware loosens or breaks more often than in a house.

Typical parts cost

Individual latches, catches, pulls, and hinges commonly run about $5 to $25 each, plus labor.

What the Shield covers

  • Cabinet latches and magnetic catches
  • Drawer pulls and knobs
  • Cabinet hinges
  • Handle screws

Not covered

  • Keys, fobs, and lock hardware
  • Cabinet doors, drawer boxes, or slides themselves
  • Damage from force or misuse

How RV and trailer rentals usually handle this

In the RV and trailer rental industry, small interior items like this are usually treated as the renter's responsibility. Rental agreements commonly list them on a damage or replacement-fee schedule and deduct the cost from the security deposit, while the larger protection plans offered with rentals are built around major physical damage and often carry deductibles higher than the repair itself. A damage waiver such as the Stress Free Shield is designed to fill that gap.

Hardware replacement is a common post-trip charge because RV latches and catches are often specialty parts sized to the cabinetry.

Avoid it: Make sure every cabinet latch clicks shut before you drive, and do not hang weight on open cabinet doors.
