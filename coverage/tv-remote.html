TV remoteNEW

A missing or broken remote control for the unit's TV.

$250 deductible per occurrenceUp to $2,500 per coverage durationParked (stationary) only

What it is

The TV remote is the handheld remote control for the television installed in the RV. RV televisions are often 12-volt models with brand-specific remotes, and remotes are one of the items most often left behind or broken.

Typical parts cost

Replacement remotes commonly run about $10 to $40. Brand-specific remotes can cost more.

Photo examples

What the Shield covers

  • A missing TV remote
  • A broken TV remote

Not covered

  • The TV, antenna, or satellite equipment
  • Audio, stereo, or other entertainment system units
  • Batteries

How RV and trailer rentals usually handle this

In the RV and trailer rental industry, small interior items like this are usually treated as the renter's responsibility. Rental agreements commonly list them on a damage or replacement-fee schedule and deduct the cost from the security deposit, while the larger protection plans offered with rentals are built around major physical damage and often carry deductibles higher than the repair itself. A damage waiver such as the Stress Free Shield is designed to fill that gap.

Missing remotes are a standard line item on rental checkout inventories and are typically charged at replacement cost.

Avoid it: Keep the remote in the same spot during your stay and check for it during your departure walkthrough.
