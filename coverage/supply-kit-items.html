Missing interior supply kit items

Small items from the unit's interior supply bin that go missing.

$250 deductible per occurrenceUp to $2,500 per coverage durationParked (stationary) only

What it is

The interior supply kit is the small bin of everyday items packed with the unit for guests to use during the trip. The Shield covers small items from that bin that are missing at checkout.

Photo examples

What the Shield covers

  • Missing small items from the interior supply bin

Not covered

  • Larger equipment and anything outside the small supply bin
  • Theft of any items
  • Consumables used up as intended

How RV and trailer rentals usually handle this

In the RV and trailer rental industry, small interior items like this are usually treated as the renter's responsibility. Rental agreements commonly list them on a damage or replacement-fee schedule and deduct the cost from the security deposit, while the larger protection plans offered with rentals are built around major physical damage and often carry deductibles higher than the repair itself. A damage waiver such as the Stress Free Shield is designed to fill that gap.

Rentals commonly check supply kits against an inventory at checkout and charge replacement cost for missing items.

Avoid it: Repack the bin before checkout and keep it where you found it.
