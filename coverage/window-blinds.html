Window blind slats or pull cords

Broken slats or pull cords on interior window blinds and shades.

$250 deductible per occurrenceUp to $2,500 per coverage durationParked (stationary) only

What it is

RV windows typically use mini blinds or pleated day and night shades that are raised and lowered with pull cords or a cord tension system. Slats can snap and cords can fray or break, especially when one side is pulled harder than the other.

Typical parts cost

Restring kits and replacement slats commonly run about $10 to $40. Full shade replacement costs more.

Photo examples

What the Shield covers

  • Accidentally broken blind slats
  • Accidentally broken or snapped pull cords

Not covered

  • Window glass, frames, and seals
  • Water damage from open vents or windows
  • Wear such as fading or sun damage

How RV and trailer rentals usually handle this

In the RV and trailer rental industry, small interior items like this are usually treated as the renter's responsibility. Rental agreements commonly list them on a damage or replacement-fee schedule and deduct the cost from the security deposit, while the larger protection plans offered with rentals are built around major physical damage and often carry deductibles higher than the repair itself. A damage waiver such as the Stress Free Shield is designed to fill that gap.

Cord and shade repairs are a frequent turnover item for rental units because shades get daily use.

Avoid it: Raise and lower shades evenly from the center rail and never yank a single cord.
